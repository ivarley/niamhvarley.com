Endless
drawing

Ten short exercises, about fifteen minutes each. They are not about making good drawings. They are about learning to look, and getting your hand to do what you meant. Nothing here keeps score.

The ten lessons

  1. 1 Lesson 1, Draw from your shoulder
  2. 2 Lesson 2, Draw without looking
  3. 3 Lesson 3, Look five times more than you draw
  4. 4 Lesson 4, Draw the holes
  5. 5 Lesson 5, Measure with your pencil
  6. 6 Lesson 6, Everything is a box, a ball, a tube, or a cone
  7. 7 Lesson 7, Draw the same dog twice
  8. 8 Lesson 8, Light on a ball
  9. 9 Lesson 9, Strokes that wrap
  10. 10 Lesson 10, One real thing, all the way

Every lesson you mark done fills in its block, and the blocks get darker as they go — that's a value scale, which is lesson 9. The gaps are the four parts of the course. Do them in any order. Nothing is locked.
