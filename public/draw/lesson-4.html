Lesson 4

Draw the holes

Skill
Negative space
You'll need
A chair, or scissors, or a mug with a handle
Time
15 minutes

Warm-up

Ten lines.

2:00

The exercise

  1. Look at a chair from the side. Find the empty shapes: the gap between the legs, the slot between the seat and the back rest, the triangle under the arm.
  2. Draw only those empty shapes. Draw no part of the chair. Fill each empty shape in solid.
  3. Stop when you've done every gap. Now look at the whole thing.

What to look for

A chair appeared, and you never drew a chair. Empty shapes are much easier to see accurately than objects, because you have no stored idea of what the gap under a chair arm is supposed to look like.

Done means

Every gap in one object is filled in.

In Endless Paper

Try it once more zoomed way in on a small object, so the gaps are huge on screen.

For the grown-up

This is the standard fix for proportion errors, and it works instantly. If a later drawing looks off and neither of you can say why, come back here and check the gaps.
