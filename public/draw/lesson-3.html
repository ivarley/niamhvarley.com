Lesson 3

Look five times more than you draw

Skill
Observation with the brakes on
You'll need
One shoe, or a sheet of paper crumpled into a ball
Time
15 minutes

Warm-up

Ten lines, ten ovals.

2:00

The exercise

  1. Put the shoe in front of you where you can see it properly.
  2. Draw it. You're allowed to look at the screen this time — but keep to the ratio: about five seconds looking at the shoe for every one second looking at your drawing.
  3. House rule: no undo. Not once, the whole lesson. A wrong line stays and you draw the right one next to it.

What to look for

Somewhere in the drawing there's a part you looked at, and a part you made up from memory. You can usually tell which is which afterwards. The made-up parts are the ones that came out fast.

Done means

One shoe drawn. Zero undos.

In Endless Paper

The undo button is right there and it's very tempting. That's why this rule exists.

For the grown-up

Undo is the enemy of observation, because it lets you correct towards the remembered symbol instead of looking again. Leaving wrong lines visible is also how professional sketches actually look. Don't tidy hers.
