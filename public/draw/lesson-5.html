Lesson 5

Measure with your pencil

Skill
Proportion
You'll need
Three objects on a table — say a mug, a book, an apple
Time
15 minutes

Warm-up

Ten ovals.

2:00

The exercise

  1. Sit still. You have to stay in the same spot the whole time or the measurements change.
  2. Hold your pencil out at full arm's length, arm locked straight. Close one eye. Line the tip of the pencil up with the top of the mug and slide your thumb down to where the bottom of the mug is. That distance on the pencil is now one mug.
  3. Keeping your thumb there, measure everything else in mugs. How many mugs wide is the book? How many mugs tall is the apple? Say the numbers out loud.
  4. Now draw all three, using the numbers rather than what you think looks right.

What to look for

At least one of the numbers will be nothing like what you expected. Things are wider, shorter, or further apart than they feel.

Done means

Three objects drawn, measured before drawn.

In Endless Paper

Use the lasso — if something ends up the wrong size, resize it instead of redrawing it. Getting the relationships right is the whole job.

For the grown-up

Arm fully locked, or the unit changes between measurements. This is called sighting and it's what life-drawing students spend the first month on.
