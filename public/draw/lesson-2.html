Lesson 2

Draw without looking

Skill
Actually seeing an edge
You'll need
Your other hand
Time
15 minutes

Warm-up

Ten fast lines from lesson 1.

2:00

The exercise

  1. Rest your other hand on the table in an interesting position.
  2. Look at your hand. Put the pencil down on the canvas. Now do not look at the screen again until you're finished. Not once.
  3. Move the pencil at exactly the speed your eye is crawling along the edge of your hand. Slow. Follow every knuckle and wrinkle.
  4. Two minutes. Then look. Then do it twice more.

What to look for

It looks completely deranged. Ignore that. Look at the detail — you recorded folds and bumps you have never drawn in your life, because for two minutes you were looking at a hand instead of remembering one.

Done means

Three attempts, no peeking.

In Endless Paper

Do the three attempts side by side so you can see them together.

For the grown-up

Blind contour is the classic intervention for symbol drawing — the habit of drawing the stored idea of a hand rather than the hand. It's also deliberately impossible to do "well," which makes it safe. Laugh at yours first and loudest.
