Lesson 10

One real thing, all the way

Skill
All of it at once
You'll need
One object you like. The lamp.
Time
30 minutes

Warm-up

Lines, ovals, value scale.

3:00

The exercise

Pick one object with some shape to it — a shoe, a teapot, a toy, a hand.

  1. Measure it first, pencil at arm's length (lesson 5).
  2. Build it out of solids, lightly (lesson 6).
  3. Look and refine the outline over the solids, five-to-one, no undo (lessons 3, 7).
  4. Check the gaps around it (lesson 4).
  5. Light it, find the five things, hatch it with strokes that wrap (lessons 8, 9).

Take the full half hour. This is the first drawing in the course that's allowed to be something you're pleased with.

Then, when it's finished: zoom right into the cast shadow of your drawing, until it fills the screen. Start a whole new world inside it. That's yours, no rules.

Done means

One object taken through all five steps.

In Endless Paper

This drawing is the front door to whatever you build inside it. The canvas doesn't end, so neither does this.

For the grown-up

Don't critique it. Ask what was hardest, or what she'd do differently, and let her lead. Keep it — put it somewhere the next ten lessons can be compared against.

---

What comes after (don't build these yet)

Perspective (one- and two-point), gesture drawing, faces and figure proportion, composition, and eventually colour — which is the point at which a different app with layers and soft brushes starts to earn its place.
